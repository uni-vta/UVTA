Camera-ring overlay assets (384 x 480).

Included:
  ring_mask.png       binary mask, uint8 0/255
  ring_patch_rgb.png  BGR ring pixels on a black background

The loader converts the texture to RGB when bgr2rgb_assets=True.

Provenance:
  The mask was produced from the camera ring in the original teleoperation
  data using SAM3 and elliptical-annulus cleanup. Ring pixels were sampled
  from 20260514_teleop/episode_0/frame_0, then manually cleaned to remove a
  lower-left artifact.

Only the mask and texture are included here; source recordings and debug
images are not distributed. Asset paths are configured in
configs/flip_book.yaml.
